Zathur Social

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Zathur Social is a social media management application operated by Zathur ("Zathur", "we", "us"). It is an internal business tool used by Zathur's own team to schedule, publish, and measure content on the social media accounts of Zathur's own brands — in the first phase, the Caeruz Facebook Page and its connected Instagram account (@caeruz_).

This policy explains what data the application accesses through Meta's platforms, why, and what rights you have in relation to it.

1. Data we access through Meta

When an authorized Zathur administrator connects a Facebook Page and its linked Instagram professional account to Zathur Social, the application accesses, through Meta's official APIs:

  • Account identification: the name and ID of the connected Facebook Page and Instagram professional account, so the application knows where to publish.
  • Administrator identification: the name and user ID of the person who authorized the connection, used only to authenticate the connection and attribute actions inside the tool.
  • Content data: the posts, captions, images, and videos that Zathur's own team creates inside the application for publication, together with their publication status.
  • Performance metrics: engagement and insights data for our own published posts (such as reach, impressions, reactions, and comments counts), used to report results to our own team.

Zathur Social only accesses data from Pages and Instagram accounts that are owned or administered by Zathur and that an administrator has explicitly connected to the application. It does not collect data about the general public, followers' private information, or accounts belonging to third parties.

2. How we use the data

  • To schedule and publish Zathur's own content to Zathur's own Pages and Instagram accounts.
  • To display publication status and performance metrics to Zathur's internal team.
  • To operate, maintain, and secure the application.

The data is used exclusively for Zathur's internal business operations. It is not used to build profiles of individuals, and it is not used for third-party advertising.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
  • We do not access private messages or content that is not required for the functions described above.

4. Sharing with service providers

Data processed by Zathur Social is stored and processed on cloud infrastructure providers that host the application on our behalf. These providers process data only as needed to run the service, under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose data when required by law or by a valid legal process.

5. Data retention

We retain connection and publication data while the corresponding account remains connected to Zathur Social and for a limited period afterwards for operational and legal records. When an account is disconnected, or upon a valid deletion request, the associated data is deleted as described in Section 7.

6. Your rights

If your personal data is processed by Zathur Social (for example, if you are an administrator who authorized a connection), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of that data at any time by contacting us at charles@zathur.com. We respond to verified requests within 30 days.

7. Data Deletion Instructions

You can have the data associated with you or with a connected Page or Instagram account deleted from Zathur Social in either of the following ways:

Option 1 — Disconnect: remove the Zathur Social app from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business Integrations / Apps and Websites) and ask our team to disconnect the account inside Zathur Social. Disconnection revokes the application's access immediately.

Option 2 — Deletion request by email: send an email to charles@zathur.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request – Zathur Social", identifying the Facebook Page and/or Instagram account concerned. We will delete the data associated with that account from Zathur Social within 30 days and confirm completion by email.

8. Security

We apply technical and organizational measures appropriate to an internal business tool, including access restricted to authorized Zathur personnel, encrypted connections, and credentials stored in access-controlled systems.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the application's functions or legal requirements change. The current version will always be available at this URL, with its effective date shown at the top.
